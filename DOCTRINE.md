# V3 — founding doctrine

**ADOPTED 2026-09-19 with AGENTS.md and ROADMAP.md. Effective and mandatory.**

## Purpose

Build complete, understandable behaviours whose interactions may produce larger
patterns. A new implementation is the owner's chosen starting point. Its value
must be demonstrated through correct accounting, clear ownership, native decision
evidence, and bounded operating costs; a blank repository proves none of these.

## Ten principles

1. **Complete the causal behaviour.** Follow an ordinary event through decision,
   execution or explicit refusal, and its consequences. Waiting, refusal, and death
   are valid outcomes; needs do not pause because a voluntary action failed.
   A positive capability claim still needs its stated successful causal chain.
   A stored field, candidate score, animation, or changed hash is insufficient.

2. **Establish opportunity before influence.** Demonstrate a consuming decision
   with meaningful alternatives before testing memory's effect. Track observation,
   eligibility, ranking, execution, and consequence separately. There is one live
   selection implementation. Probes may call it or inspect its native records;
   a parallel scorer is not evidence of what the engine chose.

3. **World design is legitimate work.** Declared exploration may vary geography,
   resource conditions, perception, consumption, and local rules within the active
   objective. Ordinary decisions must determine participants and outcomes. Do not
   script successful pairs, encounters, transfers, or reunions.

4. **Exploration is bounded; confirmation is frozen.** Declare the question,
   allowed changes, and budget before exploration. Declare activation, eligibility,
   and ranking changes before implementing them. Freeze code, configuration,
   criteria, inputs, horizon, and analysis before confirmation. Further tuning
   preserves the old result and consumes the existing budget; it cannot rescue
   an earlier result retrospectively.

5. **Correctness cannot be switched off.** Every resource gain has a source;
   production, consumption, and loss have explicit rules. Allocation, reservations,
   interruption, rejection, and completion conserve the declared quantities.
   Invalid transitions cannot commit. Domains cannot bypass settlement by writing
   balances directly. Clamping cannot conceal invalid credits.

6. **Knowledge is local and dated.** Decisions use the person's bounded view of
   the relevant tick-start state, not unrestricted world access. Retained facts
   carry observation time and a simple source identifier. Acting on stale knowledge
   may fail visibly. Do not add a general belief-revision system without a need.

7. **Evidence is native and inert.** Capture relevant inputs, eligible alternatives,
   selection, and outcome at their actual engine boundaries. Inspection cannot
   mutate simulation state. Validate instruments against real engine decisions,
   nulls, known differences, and deliberate failure cases before interpreting runs.
   Use meaningful denominators when compared trajectories diverge.

8. **Local rules are authored; patterns are findings.** A programmed preference is
   a model assumption. Cooperation, attachment, avoidance, and spatial association
   are claims to test. Absence of a pattern is a valid observation.

9. **Enforce ownership through tests and review.** Separate state, perception,
   candidate generation, selection, resolution, and evidence responsibilities.
   Test dependency direction.
   Review semantic ownership as well: correct imports do not excuse behavioural
   calculations accumulating inside person orchestration. Build only abstractions
   needed by the active slice.

10. **Evidence remains honest.** Preserve failed and superseded results. Never
    weaken assertions or replace a reference result to manufacture a pass. Label
    invalid measurements UNKNOWN for the affected claim. Distinguish a demonstrated
    mechanism, its ordinary frequency, and its robustness across conditions.

## Behavioural freedom and reference results

Capability rules may be revised through declared experiments. Mandatory invariants
may not. A reference suite checks byte equality for changes claimed to be inert.
For intentional behavioural changes, declare and explain expected differences,
retain prior results, and review new expectations. Do not preserve a correctness
defect behind an off switch merely to keep old outputs identical.

Snapshots may accelerate recovery. Replay from the initial definition and declared
inputs remains independently verifiable. State belongs to an engine instance;
isolated copies cannot share mutable state or observational references.

Use no runtime randomness through Stage 3. A genesis generator may use a named,
versioned deterministic algorithm and seed, with the resulting initial state saved.
Later randomness requires a declared modelling need, replayable state and consumption,
and an owner direction; it cannot be introduced to rescue coverage counts.

Break equivalent competing claims and partner ties by a declared, replayable rule
that rotates priority across ticks rather than permanently favouring a fixed ID.
Identity may stabilise ordering; it must not confer permanent priority. Rotation
does not prove fairness when opportunity or eligibility differs.

The new engine has no obligation to reproduce the old project's trajectories.
